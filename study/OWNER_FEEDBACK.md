# Qualitative owner feedback: real public-contract case

Collected in chat on 2026-11-11, after the owner was asked to inspect the local real SEC excerpt comparison and describe what changed and what helped or confused them, in Chinese, without timing or a scored answer.

One respondent: the project owner, not an independent recruited intended user. AI prepared the inputs and operated the test. The owner inspected the result and supplied their own feedback.

The owner reported noticing that the displayed salary moved from a number beginning with 28 to one beginning with 43. They said the interface clearly showed the change and felt it could improve their review efficiency. They also said they did not fully understand everything they saw.

This is one qualitative observation supporting the salience of the salary-change display. It does not establish a measured efficiency gain, comprehension of the full workflow, independent user validation, or legal-review accuracy.
